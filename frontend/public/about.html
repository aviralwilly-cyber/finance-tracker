About AI Finance Tracker

A personal project, not a financial service.

AI Finance Tracker is a free, open-source web app built by an independent developer named Aviral as a personal portfolio project. It is a budgeting notebook: you type in what you spent, it sorts those entries into categories, charts them, and lets you ask questions about them in plain English.

This site is not a bank. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any bank, credit union, card network, or payment processor. It does not connect to any financial institution, and it will never ask you for online banking credentials, debit or credit card numbers, account or routing numbers, a Social Insurance or Social Security number, or photo ID. If any page ever appears to ask you for those, you are not on this site — close the tab.

What it actually does

  • Records transactions that you type in yourself
  • Sorts them into spending categories and charts monthly trends
  • Tracks per-category monthly budget limits
  • Logs income and savings entries you enter by hand
  • Optionally reads a PDF bank statement that you upload yourself to save retyping, and shows you every extracted row for review before anything is saved
  • Answers questions about the figures you entered

The account it asks you to create

The sign-up form on the home page creates an ordinary login for this app and nothing else. It is handled by Firebase Authentication, a standard Google service, and consists of an email address and a password you choose. It is not a login to any bank or third-party service, and it gives the app no access to any account you hold elsewhere. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Source code

Every line of this app is public and auditable at github.com/aviralwilly-cyber/finance-tracker. Built with React, Vite, Node/Express, and Firebase.
