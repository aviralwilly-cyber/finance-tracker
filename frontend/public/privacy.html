Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

AI Finance Tracker is a personal, open-source project run by an individual developer, not a company. This page describes what the app stores and who it is shared with, in plain terms.

This app does not connect to any bank and never asks for banking credentials, card numbers, or account numbers. Everything in your account is information you typed in or uploaded yourself.

What is collected

  • Account details. The email address and password you register with. Passwords are handled entirely by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to the app or its developer.
  • Profile information. A display name, an optional phone number, an optional avatar image, and your chosen category preferences — all supplied by you.
  • Financial entries you create. Transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, categories, income records, savings records, and budget limits that you enter.
  • Uploaded statement files. If you use the PDF import feature, the file you upload is parsed to extract transaction rows. The extracted rows are shown to you for confirmation before anything is stored, and the uploaded file itself is not retained after parsing.
  • Chat history. Questions you ask the assistant and its replies, so follow-up questions have context.

Where it is stored

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Authentication), under a per-user record keyed to your account. Security rules restrict each record to the account that created it.

Who it is shared with

Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with advertisers. There is no advertising or third-party tracking on this site. Data reaches exactly two outside services:

  • Google Firebase — hosting, authentication, and the database.
  • Groq — the AI provider. When you use categorization, chat, quick-add, statement extraction, or budget nudges, the relevant text (for example a transaction description, or the figures needed to answer your question) is sent to Groq's API to generate the response. Do not enter anything into this app that you would not be comfortable sending to a third-party AI provider.

Your controls

Under Settings → Account & security you can change your email or password, export everything in your account as a JSON file, and permanently delete your account along with its data.

Security and expectations

This is a hobby project, offered as-is with no warranty and no service guarantee. Reasonable care has been taken — per-user data isolation, authenticated API routes, no credential collection — but it has not been independently audited, and you should not treat it as you would a regulated financial service. Please use a password unique to this site, and consider whether you want to enter genuinely sensitive figures at all.

Contact

Privacy questions or security reports: open an issue at github.com/aviralwilly-cyber/finance-tracker/issues.
